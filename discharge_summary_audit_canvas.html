Materials and Methods
1

Study design and setting

This study was a retrospective clinical audit of provisional discharge summaries generated by the Department of General Medicine at Santosh Medical College and Hospital, Ghaziabad, India. The primary objective was to evaluate documentation quality and completeness using a standardized scoring rubric.

2

Data source and selection criteria

The audit population consisted of provisional discharge summaries generated during the June and July 2026 clinical periods.

  • Inclusion: All provisional discharge summaries from General Medicine wards.
  • Exclusion: Drafts, duplicate summaries and superseded documents.
  • Where multiple versions existed for one IPD number, the latest final version based on the system timestamp was retained.

Final cohort: 91 unique summaries from 109 screened files.

3

Auditing and grading rubric

Each summary was assessed against nine clinical-documentation parameters. Each parameter was graded 0, 1 or 2: 0 indicated absent or poor documentation, 1 indicated incomplete or vague documentation, and 2 indicated complete and clear documentation. The maximum score was 18.

4

Technical extraction and auditing

Clinical data extraction and preliminary scoring were performed using an agentic artificial-intelligence pipeline utilizing a multimodal large language model, Gemini 3.5 Flash.

Each discharge summary was individually reviewed by Dr. Anupam, who added general comments and cross-checked the agentic output, creating a human-in-the-loop audit process.

  • Native page-by-page PDF viewing preserved document layout.
  • The audit list was evaluated concurrently by nine parallel subagents.
  • Scores, comments and totals were consolidated into a standardized Excel database.

Improvement plan

Immediate

Structured medication instructions

Record drug, strength, dose, route, frequency, duration, indication and relation to previous treatment.

Immediate

Mandatory warning symptoms

Include disease-specific red flags, emergency contact details and where the patient should report.

Immediate

Pending-report workflow

Document “none pending” or list the investigation, expected date, responsible clinician and communication plan.

High

Objective discharge condition

Record vital signs, oxygen need, sensorium, feeding, mobility and devices in situ.

High

Separate LAMA and transfer templates

Create a mandatory minimum safety dataset for non-routine discharges.

Medium

Audit manual and reliability testing

Define examples for scores 0, 1 and 2 and double-audit 10–20% of summaries.
