ASCVD & PREVENT Risk Calculator

Compare the 2013 Pooled Cohort Equation with the AHA PREVENT-ASCVD equation

Overview

This calculator estimates 10-year risk of a first atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease event using two models:

  • PCE / traditional ASCVD risk: age 40–79 years.
  • PREVENT-ASCVD: age 30–79 years, with kidney and treatment variables included.
  • Personalized PREVENT: adds UACR and HbA1c when both are entered. Social Deprivation Index is treated as unavailable using the published missing-SDI term.

For South Asian adults, the PCE result uses the White/Other equation; neither model has been specifically calibrated in an Indian cohort.

🔒 Calculations run locally in your browser. No patient data are transmitted.

    Risk categories and interpretation

    PCE categories: low <5%; borderline 5–<7.5%; intermediate 7.5–<20%; high ≥20%.

    PREVENT-ASCVD categories: low <3%; borderline 3–<5%; intermediate 5–<10%; high ≥10%.

    The category crosswalk comes from the 2026 ACC/AHA dyslipidemia guideline. A calculated percentage supports—but does not replace—clinical judgment, evaluation of risk enhancers, coronary artery calcium where appropriate, or disease-specific treatment recommendations.

    Method, limitations and equation sources
    • PCE uses age, sex, total cholesterol, HDL-C, systolic BP, BP treatment, smoking and diabetes. For South Asian adults this page applies the White/Other equation because no Indian-specific PCE equation exists.
    • PREVENT base uses age, sex, non-HDL-C, HDL-C, systolic BP, diabetes, smoking, eGFR, BP treatment and statin use, including published interaction and spline terms.
    • Personalized PREVENT uses the published full 10-year ASCVD model with UACR and HbA1c. Because US Social Deprivation Index is unavailable, the model’s published missing-SDI indicator is used.
    • The calculator does not establish treatment eligibility by itself. Diabetes, CKD, familial hypercholesterolemia, LDL-C ≥190 mg/dL, established ASCVD and other conditions may require action independent of the displayed estimate.
    • PREVENT was developed in US adults and PCE was developed primarily in US White and Black cohorts. Both require validation and calibration before being treated as precise event probabilities in North Indian populations.
