Clinical Audit Dashboard

Overview

The Clinical Audit Dashboard is an academic and clinical quality improvement initiative led by the Department of General Medicine at Santosh Medical College and Hospital, Ghaziabad. Under the supervision of Dr. Anupam Kumar Singh MD, this platform integrates parallel agentic AI audit workflows with rigorous clinician validation to assess and optimize documentation quality and patient-safety communication on discharge.

  • Retrospective Discharge Summary Audit (91 Summaries)

    A retrospective audit of 91 unique discharge summaries evaluating documentation completeness against a standardized 9-parameter rubric (maximum score of 18). Parameters include etiologic diagnosis specificity, hospital course individualization, drug dosing/duration completeness, pending report notation, and objective discharge vitals. This report highlights key clinical documentation deficiencies and lists systemic recommendations for electronic medical records (EMR) improvement.

  • Diabetes Discharge-Care & Prescribing Safety Audit (57 Patients)

    A targeted clinical safety audit of 57 patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) focusing on chronic kidney disease (CKD) screening, antidiabetic prescribing safety, and patient education. Highlights critical safety indicators including spot Urine Albumin Creatinine Ratio (UACR) screening rates, inappropriate renal prescribing, and unsafe sulfonylurea (Glimepiride/Gliclazide) use in septic or elderly patients.

    57 Patients UACR Screening Prescribing Safety
  • Heart Failure Discharge Bundle Audit (31 Patients)

    A targeted clinical quality and safety audit of 31 patients with heart failure or cardiomyopathy assessing adherence to Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) bundles (ACEi/ARB/ARNI, beta-blockers, MRAs, SGLT2i). Displays Ejection Fraction (EF) classification, precipitating factor identification, salt/fluid restriction advice, daily weight monitoring, and post-discharge renal follow-up rates, along with co-prescription rates for Aspirin and Statins.

    31 Patients GDMT Bundle Renal & Potassium Safety
  • Antimicrobial Stewardship Quality Audit (EMR Summaries)

    A targeted quality improvement audit assessing antimicrobial prescribing practices. Focuses on key compliance criteria including documented indication, dose completeness, choice appropriateness, therapy duration, and culture-directed de-escalation. Highlights opportunities for enhancing antibiotic stewardship and compliance with hospital guidelines.
