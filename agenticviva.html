Agentic Clinical Viva

Overview

Agentic Clinical Viva is an experimental assessment framework where a student's clinical case presentation is evaluated simultaneously by two leading AI models (ChatGPT and Gemini). This side-by-side viva explores:

  • Clinical Localization: The models' ability to identify the anatomical site of the lesion from history alone.
  • Physical Examination: Critical appraisal of the examination findings and their correlation with the suspected etiology.
  • Textbook Alignment: Each round is scored and referenced against standard medical texts like Kundu and Alagappan.

Supervised by Anupam Singh MD, this platform highlights the comparative strengths of AI in medical education and clinical reasoning.

  • Mr. B.: Decompensated Liver Disease, Ascites, and Jaundice Viva

    A 49-year-old male with a 25-year history of heavy alcohol intake presents with 1 month of progressive bilateral pedal edema, 10 days of deep icterus, 8 days of tense abdominal distension, and 4–5 days of oliguria. Physical examination demonstrates stigmata of chronic liver failure (icterus, spider naevi, palmar erythema, parotid enlargement), tense ascites with fluid thrill and shifting dullness, and signs of impending hepatorenal dysfunction. This agentic clinical viva evaluates the comparative diagnostic localization, risk-factor quantification, physical examination critique, and textbook-aligned scoring across ChatGPT and Gemini.

  • Mr. A.S.: Cerebellar Ataxia, Dysarthria, and Cognitive Decline Viva

    A 48-year-old male daily wage worker with a 20-year history of heavy alcohol intake presents with 2–3 months of progressive broad-based ataxic gait (falling towards the left), scanning/staccato dysarthria, urinary frequency, and frontoparietal headache. Examination reveals an MMSE of 22/30, prominent appendicular and midline cerebellar signs, and past antiparkinsonian drug trials. This agentic viva evaluates the AI models' clinical localization, reasoning across alcoholic cerebellar degeneration, NPH, MSA-C, and textbook-aligned scoring.

  • Mr. V.K.: Acute Vertigo, Diplopia, and Cerebellar Signs Viva

    A 76-year-old male with a 10-year history of hypertension presents with 1 day of sudden-onset vertigo (surroundings spinning), binocular horizontal diplopia, and slurred speech (dysarthria). Physical examination reveals a right medial rectus palsy and right-sided appendicular cerebellar ataxia. This agentic viva evaluates the AI models' localization of a suspected vertebrobasilar/brainstem-cerebellar vascular event, oculomotor nerve appraisal, and textbook-aligned scoring.

  • The Progressive Weakness Viva

    A 47-year-old labourer presents with 4 months of progressive back pain, paraplegia, and bladder dysfunction. This agentic viva evaluates the AI models' ability to localise the anatomical site of the lesion and identify diagnostic milestones in a resource-limited setting.

  • Mrs. D.: Acute Headache, Vertigo, and Monoparesis Viva

This agentic viva evaluates the AI models' approach to chronic respiratory illness, grade 2 clubbing, left hemithorax dullness with diminished breath sounds (pleural effusion vs. consolidation), and risk assessment for underlying malignancy versus tuberculosis.

  • Acute Wake-up Stroke with Right Hemiparesis and Dysarthria Viva

This agentic viva evaluates the AI models' localization reasoning between capsular/MCA vs. brainstem (pontine) syndromes, appraisal of neurological examination discrepancies, and textbook-aligned scoring.
