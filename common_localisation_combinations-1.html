Common Localisation Combinations

Finding clusters that localise together · Combination → Structure → Why it fits
Curated by Anupam Singh MD, Professor, Department of Medicine, Santosh Medical College
Teaching rule: one sign suggests a structure, two signs localise a lesion, and three concordant signs often make the localisation hard to miss. Use this sheet after the structure-based and symptom-based tables, but before working through full cases.
