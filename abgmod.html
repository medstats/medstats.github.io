Overall interpretation
Clinical method used
  • pH: acidemia < 7.35; alkalemia > 7.45.
  • Metabolic acidosis compensation: Winter's formula, expected PaCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2.
  • Metabolic alkalosis compensation: expected rise in PaCO₂ ≈ 0.6–0.75 mmHg per 1 mEq/L rise in HCO₃⁻; the app uses 0.7 × ΔHCO₃⁻ + 40 with a conservative ±5 mmHg interpretation range.
  • Respiratory disorders use acute/chronic bicarbonate compensation ranges; if onset is unknown, both are shown.
  • Anion gap = Na − (Cl + HCO₃⁻); corrected AG = AG + 2.5 × (4 − albumin).
  • Delta ratio (only when AG is elevated): <0.4 predominantly NAGMA; 0.4–0.8 mixed HAGMA + NAGMA; 0.8–2 predominantly HAGMA; >2 HAGMA + metabolic alkalosis/elevated baseline HCO₃⁻.
  • Internal consistency: ΔpH ≤0.02 is treated as consistent; >0.02 triggers a warning, with >0.05 considered significant.
  • Alveolar gas equation: PAO₂ = FiO₂ × (PB − 47) − PaCO₂/R.
This tool is intended for clinicians and medical education. It should not be used as the sole basis for diagnosis or treatment. Always interpret ABG results with the clinical context, sample type, laboratory reference ranges, ventilator settings, and altitude.
