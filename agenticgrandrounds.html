Agentic Grand Rounds

About This Initiative

Welcome to Agentic Grand Rounds. This showcase features real clinical cases solved through a staged, multi-role discussion framework simulating a hospital medical team:

  • Gemini (Resident): Initial case analysis, differential diagnosis, and proposed investigations.
  • Grok (Attending): Critical appraisal of the resident's plan, Bayesian refinement, and focus on "don't miss" diagnoses.
  • ChatGPT / Kimi (Expert): Unifying diagnosis, key pivot identification, and high-yield teaching pearls.

This "Human-in-the-loop" framework (supervised by Anupam Singh MD) aims to sharpen diagnostic precision and explore the nuances of medical logic in a transparent, collaborative environment.

  • The Refractory Tongue: A Non-Healing Ulcer in a Man with Progressive Weakness and Hypoxemia

    A 74-year-old man on escalating immunosuppression for refractory ocular symptoms presents with dyspnea, proximal weakness, hypoxemia, a painful ulcerative tongue lesion, diarrhea, and collapsing blood counts. A high-stakes dissection of severe lymphopenia, a destructive oral mucosal process, and the discipline of re-biopsying a lesion that refuses to heal.

  • The Endocrine Disconnect: Asymptomatic Hyperkalemia, Dual RAAS Suppression, Normal GFR, and the 1800 mmol Natriuresis Paradox

    A 34-year-old normotensive woman presents for routine evaluation with incidental, reproducible hyperkalemia (K+ 5.8 mmol/L), normal bicarbonate, and pristine renal function (eGFR 121 mL/min/1.73 m²). Endocrine workup reveals a profound physiological contradiction: complete suppression of plasma renin activity and aldosterone, alongside massive 24-hour urinary electrolyte excretion (Na+ 1800 mmol/day, K+ 306 mmol/day). A high-stakes dissection of the adrenal volume-vs-potassium hierarchy, aldosterone-independent kaliuresis, and hidden dietary exposures.

  • The Traveler's Surge: Diarrhea, Massive Leukocytosis (145,400/µL), Subendocardial LGE, and Cytoreductive Cure

    A 27-year-old graduate student with treated ulcerative colitis presents with 2 weeks of diarrhea, fatigue, and an extraordinary white cell count of 145,400/µL (83% differential). Complicated by gastroenteritis, acute systolic heart failure (LVEF 26%), subendocardial LGE, and diffuse alveolar hemorrhage. A high-stakes dissection of multi-line refractoriness, cytoreduction, and allogeneic stem-cell transplantation.

  • The Young Carpenter's Collapse: Macrocytic Pancytopenia, Night Sweats, Vitiligo, and Extreme LDH Surge

    A 21-year-old carpenter with a history of vitiligo presents with 4 weeks of fatigue, night sweats, orthostatic light-headedness, severe macrocytic pancytopenia (Hb 5.1 g/dL, MCV 105.9 fL), LDH surge (>2,500 U/L), and undetectable haptoglobin. A high-stakes dissection of neutrophil hypersegmentation, autoantibodies, and reticulocyte recovery.

  • The Floor Collapse: Severe Mechanical Back Pain, Leukoerythroblastosis, Ferritin Surge, and Misleading TTP

    A 65-year-old woman presents with severe axial back pain, 9 kg weight loss, acute floor collapse, pancytopenia (platelets 10,000/µL), extreme ferritin surge (>24,000 µg/L), and leukoerythroblastosis. Transferred for suspected TTP, but normal haptoglobin (91 mg/dL) forces a re-evaluation. A high-stakes dissection of myelophthisis, neuroendocrine IHC, and palliative transition.

  • Thinking Outside the Heart: Late-Onset Asthma, Severe Troponin Surge, MINOCA, and Pericardial Effusion

    A 50-year-old woman with 1-year late-onset asthma presents with exertional pleuritic chest pain post-vaccination. Labs reveal extreme hypereosinophilia (5,820/µL), troponin T surge (780 ng/L), normal coronaries, and MRI showing subendocardial LGE and edema. A high-stakes dissection of ANCA-negative myopericarditis, endomyocardial biopsy, and IL-1 blockade.

  • The Biphasic Relapse: Stress-Triggered Cytopenias, Transient Aphasia, Schistocytosis, and the Biomarker Paradox

  • The Postpartum Bruise: Spontaneous Ecchymoses, Hemarthroses, and the Incubated Coagulation Assay
