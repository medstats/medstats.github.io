Clinical Audit: Heart Failure Discharge Bundle

Audit Overview

This dashboard presents a retrospective clinical quality audit of 31 patients diagnosed with Heart Failure (HF) or Cardiomyopathy discharged from the Department of General Medicine at Santosh Medical College and Hospital, Ghaziabad.

The audit evaluated documentation completeness and adherence to Guideline-Directed Medical Therapy (GDMT) including Ejection Fraction (EF) documentation, HF classification, OHA class reconciliation, and medication safety practices at discharge.

Materials and Methods
1

Audit cohort & setting

A retrospective clinical audit of 31 patients with heart failure or cardiomyopathy admitted to the Department of General Medicine at Santosh Medical College and Hospital, Ghaziabad during June–July 2026.

2

Data extraction pipeline

Heart failure cases were identified via diagnostic keywords (CHF, CCF, HFrEF, HFmrEF, HFpEF, DCMP, Cardiomyopathy, LV Dysfunction). PDFs were extracted to text via pdftotext -layout. Audit extraction was performed in parallel using four automated subagents running Gemini 3.5 Flash.

3

Active vs Non-standard Discharges

Medication analysis was restricted to 29 active discharges. One patient who expired in hospital (IP260613159) and one who Left Against Medical Advice (LAMA, IP260627006) were excluded from the prescribing denominators.

4

GDMT Class Mapping

Prescriptions were resolved using the local directory (kb.txt) to map commercial brands to drug classes (e.g. Vymada to ARNI, Dytor/Dytor Plus to loop diuretics/MRAs, Met XL/Metolar to beta-blockers, Oxra/Dapa to SGLT2i).

Heart Failure Quality Improvement Action Plan

Immediate

Standardized Discharge Vitals & Weight

Require documentation of discharge heart rate, blood pressure, and weight to ensure hemodynamic stability prior to ward discharge.

Immediate

GDMT Checklists

Integrate a "GDMT Discharge Checklist" in EMR templates to prompt clinicians on prescribing ARNI/ACEi, Beta-blockers, MRAs, and SGLT2 inhibitors.

High Priority

Lab follow-up Alerts

Automate laboratory order templates for follow-up Serum Creatinine and Electrolytes (K+) within 7–10 days of starting/adjusting MRAs or loop diuretics.

High Priority

Structured Fluid & Salt Advice

Add pre-configured discharge advice blocks detailing salt restriction (< 2g/day) and fluid restriction (1.5L/day) based on clinical congestion.

High Priority

Daily Weight Tracking Sheets

Provide patients with a "Heart Failure Self-Care Sheet" to record daily morning weights, with clear red flags (e.g. weight gain > 2kg in 3 days).

Immediate

Cardiology OPD Reviews

Ensure all patients diagnosed with HFrEF or valvular/ischemic cardiomyopathy have a scheduled review with Cardiology OPD within 14 days of discharge.
