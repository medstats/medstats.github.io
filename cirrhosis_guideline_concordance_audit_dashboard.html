Guideline Concordance in Inpatient Documentation of Decompensated Cirrhosis

Retrospective Quality-of-Care Audit

This dashboard summarizes 235 unique admissions selected from 2615 total admissions. Clinical phenotypes and process measures were extracted from DIAGNOSIS, COURSE DURING HOSPITALISATION, TREATMENT GIVEN, ADVICE ON DISCHARGE and OTHER INVESTIGATIONS. Course of hospital stay was treated as a core evidence source for procedures, complications, treatment evolution and clinical context.

Cohort-definition note
The exact cohort used in the analysis is diagnosis-defined CLD/cirrhosis, not a strict decompensation-only cohort. Domain-specific denominators identify decompensated phenotypes (ascites, HE, probable variceal bleeding, SBP, HRS). Therefore the requested title is retained, but the base-cohort inclusion should be described this way in any manuscript.

Cohort Selection Filters Used
1

Source and analytical unit

Started from master_discharge_sections_all.csv. The analytical unit was one hospital admission, identified by IP Number / AdmissionNo. Duplicate admission summaries were removed so each IP Number contributed once.

2

Primary inclusion filter

Admissions were included when the DIAGNOSIS contained explicit terminology consistent with chronic liver disease/cirrhosis, including CLD, DCLD, chronic liver disease/chronic parenchymal liver disease, cirrhosis, or portal-hypertension/variceal terminology used as a cirrhotic phenotype. The saved 235 IP Numbers define the exact reproducible analysis cohort.

3

What was not required for base inclusion

A patient did not have to demonstrate ascites, HE, variceal bleeding, SBP or HRS to enter the 235-admission base cohort. These complications were derived subsequently from diagnosis and hospital-course text and used to define domain-specific eligible denominators.

4

Clinical-text evidence sources

COURSE DURING HOSPITALISATION was searched together with DIAGNOSIS, TREATMENT GIVEN, ADVICE ON DISCHARGE and OTHER INVESTIGATIONS for paracentesis, endoscopy/EVL, HE precipitants, albumin, vasoactive drugs, vasoconstrictors, ICU transfer and contraindication clues. Course text did not independently expand the saved base cohort.

Audit Interpretation Rules

Eligible denominators

Each criterion was calculated only in the clinically relevant phenotype wherever possible. Examples: paracentesis in ascites; lactulose in HE; vasoactive therapy/endoscopy in probable variceal bleeding; albumin in SBP/HRS.

Exceptions and contraindications

Medication absence was not automatically labelled inappropriate. Course/diagnosis text was searched for AKI/HRS, hypotension, transfer, LAMA and other context. Remaining negative findings are reported as documentation or possible process gaps requiring clinical review.

Readmission

Readmission is based on repeat admissions to this hospital using UMR Number and therefore does not capture admissions elsewhere.

Data limitation

Discharge-summary documentation is an imperfect proxy for bedside care. Procedures or drugs may have been delivered but omitted from the final summary; manual adjudication is required before attributing a true care failure.

Quality Improvement Priorities

Immediate

Ascites Admission Bundle

Add a structured admission prompt for diagnostic paracentesis eligibility and ascitic-fluid testing, with a documented reason when not performed.

Immediate

Variceal Bleed Bundle

Use a checklist covering vasoactive therapy, antibiotic prophylaxis, endoscopy/EVL and secondary prophylaxis planning.

High Priority

HE Discharge Bundle

Document likely precipitant, lactulose plan, bowel-movement target and rifaximin indication for recurrent/persistent HE.

High Priority

SBP/HRS Checklist

Standardize albumin and vasoconstrictor eligibility documentation and record contraindications or alternative diagnoses.

High Priority

Portal HTN Prophylaxis

At discharge, record NSBB eligibility, treatment, contraindication or endoscopic alternative in patients with relevant portal-hypertension phenotypes.

High Priority

Surveillance & Referral

Add discharge fields for HCC-surveillance status and transplant-evaluation/referral consideration after decompensation.
