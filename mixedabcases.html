ABG Teaching Cases

Mixed Acid–Base Disorders

Ten worked clinical vignettes using the same 8-step sequence as the ABG Analyzer: internal consistency → pH → primary process → expected compensation → anion gap → albumin correction/delta ratio → mixed-disorder check → oxygenation.

Case index

Each case is internally consistent and intentionally constructed to demonstrate a specific mixed disorder.

Calculation rules used

  • Metabolic acidosis: Winter's formula — expected PaCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2 mmHg.
  • Metabolic alkalosis: expected PaCO₂ rises about 0.6–0.75 mmHg per 1 mEq/L rise in HCO₃⁻.
  • Respiratory acidosis: HCO₃⁻ rises about 1–2 mEq/L per 10 mmHg PaCO₂ acutely and 3–4 chronically.
  • Respiratory alkalosis: HCO₃⁻ falls about 1–2 mEq/L per 10 mmHg PaCO₂ acutely and 4–5 chronically.
  • Corrected AG = AG + 2.5 × (4 − albumin). Delta ratio = (corrected AG − normal AG)/(24 − HCO₃⁻).
  • Delta ratio: <0.4 predominantly NAGMA; 0.4–0.8 mixed HAGMA + NAGMA; 0.8–2 predominantly HAGMA; >2 HAGMA + metabolic alkalosis/elevated baseline HCO₃⁻.

Educational examples only. Formula cutoffs are approximations and should be interpreted with the clinical setting, timing of samples, treatment already given, albumin, renal function and laboratory method.
