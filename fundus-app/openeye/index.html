Technical description

Model and preprocessing
ItemDetail
ModelOPENEye-FM vision branch (yangzhou12/OPENEye-FM), 36 disease and sign outputs. MIT licence. 371 MB ONNX, cached on the device after the first load.
PreprocessingResize 256 with bicubic interpolation, centre crop 224, normalisation with channel means 0.3156, 0.2095, 0.1650 and standard deviations 0.1364, 0.0858, 0.0844.
OutputRaw logits. No thresholds, calibration or class balancing are applied, so ranks and relative scores are meaningful while absolute values are not probabilities.
How to read the list

Scores are unnormalised model outputs ranked highest first. A higher score means the model associates the image more strongly with that label; it is not a probability and does not indicate the presence of disease. Several related labels often appear together because the model was trained with overlapping disease and sign categories.

Do not compare absolute scores between different photographs: the model has no per-image calibration.

Optimal input

A single colour fundus photograph of the posterior pole, JPEG or PNG. Full-frame NIDEK photographs work directly; the model expects a centred retina, so cropped or off-centre images shift the scores.
