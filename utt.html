Top 15 Takeaways for Clinicians

  1. Definition of Congenital TTP (Upshaw-Schulman Syndrome): Congenital TTP (cTTP) is an ultra-rare autosomal recessive disorder caused by biallelic pathogenic mutations in the ADAMTS13 gene, resulting in severe hereditary deficiency of von Willebrand factor-cleaving protease (<10% activity).
  2. Pregnancy as a Major Unmasking Trigger: Pregnancy increases endothelial stress, plasma volume, and circulating ultra-large vWF multimers, frequently unmasking previously silent cTTP during the second/third trimester or postpartum period.
  3. Diagnostic Triad of Thrombotic Microangiopathy: Microangiopathic Hemolytic Anemia (MAHA), severe thrombocytopenia, and microvascular end-organ damage (renal, neurological, cardiac) define TMA, requiring immediate clinical recognition.
  4. Profound Thrombocytopenia Hallmark: Platelet counts below 30,000/µL (and often <10,000/µL) strongly favor TTP over preeclampsia, HELLP syndrome, or complement-mediated atypical HUS.
  5. Normal Coagulation Profile: Coagulation parameters (PT/INR, aPTT, and Fibrinogen) remain normal in TTP, distinguishing microthrombotic microangiopathies from Disseminated Intravascular Coagulation (DIC).
  6. Utility of the PLASMIC Score: A PLASMIC score of 6 or 7 (platelets <30k, hemolysis, no cancer/transplant, MCV <90 fL, INR <1.5, Cr <2.0 mg/dL) predicts severe ADAMTS13 deficiency with >90% sensitivity and specificity, justifying emergency plasma exchange.
  7. Severe ADAMTS13 Deficiency Diagnostic Threshold: Plasma ADAMTS13 activity <10% (or <0.10 IU/mL) confirms the diagnosis of TTP.
  8. Inhibitor-Negative Pearl for Congenital Etiology: Severe ADAMTS13 deficiency (<10%) paired with a negative inhibitor antibody (<0.4 U/mL) should immediately prompt genetic sequencing of the ADAMTS13 gene to diagnose cTTP.
  9. Emergency Therapeutic Plasma Exchange (PEX): Daily PEX or fresh frozen plasma (FFP) infusion must be initiated urgently upon clinical suspicion of TMA without waiting for send-out ADAMTS13 results.
  10. Futile Corticosteroid De-escalation: Once cTTP is confirmed by negative autoantibody testing and genetic analysis, immunosuppressive therapy (steroids, rituximab) should be promptly discontinued as the disease is non-immune.
  11. Platelet Transfusion Contraindication: Platelet transfusions are strictly contraindicated in TTP due to the risk of precipitating acute microvascular thrombosis, except in life-threatening hemorrhage.
  12. Role of Recombinant ADAMTS13 (Adzynma): Recombinant ADAMTS13 (aphadamts13 / Adzynma) or regular FFP infusions provide definitive replacement therapy for patients with cTTP.
  13. Preconception & Pregnancy Prophylaxis Protocol: Women with cTTP require prophylactic FFP or recombinant ADAMTS13 infusions starting preconception or in the first trimester to prevent intrauterine fetal demise (IUFD) and maternal TMA flares.
  14. Autosomal Recessive Cascade Screening: Genetic counseling and targeted ADAMTS13 mutation screening should be offered to all first-degree relatives and future reproductive partners.
  15. Long-Term Remission Surveillance: Post-recovery monitoring includes serial ADAMTS13 activity levels, complete blood counts, and urine protein-to-creatinine ratios to maintain plasma ADAMTS13 activity above 10–20%.
