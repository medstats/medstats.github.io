Model & Processing Details

Architecture & Scope

FastUGI-Net (B7+XXS) — dual-head convolutional-attention network trained for upper GI endoscopy landmark localization (9 anatomical sites: esophagus, squamocolumnar junction, fundus, gastric body antegrade/retroflex, angulus, antrum, duodenal bulb, descending duodenum) and 10 disease categories (normal, esophageal neoplasm/varices, GERD, gastric neoplasm/polyp/ulcer/varices, duodenal bulb & descending duodenal disease).

Preprocessing & Privacy

Opaque JPEG/PNG images (up to 24 MP, 12,000 px/side, 64 MB) are resized via separable Keys bicubic interpolation (224 × 224) with EXIF orientation applied and embedded color-profile conversion disabled. Processing runs locally in a background Web Worker; checking offline cache…
